# Michael Duncan McArthur

I'm Principal at 4th Culture in Toronto. Since 1999 I've designed digital products. These days agents do much of the building under my direction.

## What I'm working on

- [4th Culture](https://4thcltr.com/): an independent practice built to make technology more expressive and recognizably human without surrendering judgment
- [Heuristic analyses](https://heuristics.4thcltr.com/) of well known sites and identified patterns
- [Papers](https://papers.4thcltr.com/), including the [AI Use Levels](https://papers.4thcltr.com/ai-use-levels/) spec
- [BinderCurve](https://bindercurve.com/): fresh free trading card game community tools
- [MoonMan](https://playmoonman.com/): a minimalist gravity escape game with multi-platform support
- [gorfed.net](https://gorfed.net/): my personal hub, portfolio and archive since 2002

## Design history

[gorfed.net/design](https://gorfed.net/design/) lists 12 awards and 200+ clients and organizations.

- TD (2018 to 2026): Experience Design Lead, then Manager, Experience Design in TD Invent's Human-Centered Design Practice. Led design teams across Wealth, on WebBroker, Web Business Banking, TD Asset Management and EasyWeb.
- The Globe and Mail ranked TD Direct Investing Canada's top digital brokerage in 2023, 2024 and 2025
- Co-inventor on 2 related patents filed at TD to protect investors from predatory short sellers
- Klick (2016 to 2018): Director of User Experience, then Director of Product Design. UX on Circulation, a health-transport platform acquired in 2018 for $57.5M. Then directed product design on KINETiQ.
- TowIt (2014 to 2015): co-founded a civic app, named one of the 12 best apps made in Canada (Techvibes, 2015)
- 2005 to 2014: Mindblossom, Chiqpea, Mouth Media, Blast Radius and BNOTIONS. Clients included BMW, Nike, Microsoft and American Express.
- Boardwise (1999 to 2001): founded and ran the first online skateboard shop in the Middle East, out of Dubai

## Development history

gorfed.net began in 2002 as The Gorfed Network, a self-release imprint for my music. Today it's a static site I build myself. I design each product and direct the build, front end and back end.

Shipped in 2026:

- BinderCurve: tools for 10 trading card games, on the web
- MoonMan: storyboards from 2013, now on the App Store and Google Play
- PromptBoi: a playful feed of AI wins, fails and links
- [ACID2Reaper](https://github.com/gorfednet/ACID2REAPER): open source, beta 0.2. Converts ACID projects into REAPER sessions. Tested on 245 real projects.

## Infrastructure

I host my network of sites myself, with encrypted off-site backups every night. The shared CI and tooling are public in [gorfednet/.github](https://github.com/gorfednet/.github).

- CI on a self-hosted Linux pool
- Reusable checks, a verification kit and an AI reviewer on pull requests
- A merge bot (ship-bot) for reviewed and green pull requests, plus a daily janitor and a fleet status report
- Local AI agent sessions build under 1 control session I direct
- [AI Use Levels](https://github.com/gorfednet/ai-use-levels) tags on commits and pages
